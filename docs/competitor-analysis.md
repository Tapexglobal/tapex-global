# Competitor research — 26 October 2026

Method: reviewed current public pages through web browsing/search, followed by bounded 390×844 Chrome observations. Observations describe visible information architecture and content, not independently verified competitor claims. Complete mobile usability, Lighthouse scores and full technical crawls of competitors were not measured; do not infer accessibility or performance from appearance. No competitor branding, copy, stories or statistics are reused.

## Search intent findings
Searches covered study-abroad consultants in Hyderabad/Warangal, India-to-UK/Australia/Canada/Germany study requirements and accommodation/arrival support. Results include large counsellor networks, regional consultancies, university country-entry pages and official requirements resources. This supports separate counselling, destination research and practical-support intents; it is qualitative evidence, not search-volume or ranking data.

The implementation prioritizes the assessment conversation, course/budget questions, institution-specific requirements and optional practical support. Each country page has unique sections and official references. No mass-produced city pages, job placement hub, unverified partnerships or offer campaigns are retained.

## Verification limits
Crawler-readable headings and topic links were observed in returned HTML. Metadata was checked on accessible mobile pages only. Schema validity, redirects beyond observed brand redirects, mobile keyboard behavior and Core Web Vitals were not comprehensively audited. TAPEX’s own implementation receives direct browser and automated checks; competitor performance comparisons are intentionally not scored.
