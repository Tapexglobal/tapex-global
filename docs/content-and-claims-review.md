## Official content references
Country copy avoids fixed fees, earnings, work-hour allowances or visa durations. It directs students to current requirements and explains institution-specific checks. Reviewed sources:

- [UK Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa) and [Graduate visa](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa).
- [Australian Student visa](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500), [Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/) and [CRICOS](https://cricos.education.gov.au/). Home Affairs content is dynamic; no detailed eligibility table was reproduced from it.
- [Canada study permits](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit.html). DLI and PGWP resources are linked for separate current eligibility checks.
- [DAAD requirements](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/) and [requirements overview](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/overview/).
- Current university entry examples show why universal criteria are inappropriate: [Manchester India guidance](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/international/country-specific-information/india/) and [Melbourne India guidance](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/connect-with-us/international/india). Neither is represented as a TAPEX partner.

## Review workflow
Assign a business content owner and destination reviewers before launch. Monthly, and before any ad campaign, check official sources and institution pages; record reviewer, date, exact scope and changed requirements. Immediately correct material changes. Review intake copy per campaign rather than inserting year-based urgency. Store testimonial/offer approvals outside the public repository. Record contact, service, pricing and legal approvals in a private launch checklist.
